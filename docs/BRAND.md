# WolfPit brand

Chicago pit, not a meme wolf. Bone ivory on near-black. Antique brass for the inner ring only.

No purple. No neon. No howling-at-the-moon wolves.

## Mark

Concentric **octagon** = the pit, seen from above. Cut-steel **wolf** in the well. The inner ring is brass.

Type: IBM Plex Sans. Wordmark tracking wide, like an exchange board. Never italic. Never “Wolf Pit” as two cutesy words in a logo.

## Clear space

One octagon-width of black around the mark. Do not put it on purple, green, or a photo of a wolf.

## Voice

Pit. Term. Covered. Never naked. No “yield.” No 10,000% APR.
